What Stickies is

Stickies is a free, open-source personal sticky-note and organization tool. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no company, account, or service behind it beyond the code itself.

Your data

Your sticky contents, settings, and other app data are stored locally in your browser's storage on your own device. Nobody else can access this data — it never passes through any server operated by the developer.

If you choose to connect Google Drive sync, your Stickies data is copied only to your own Google Drive account, using access that is limited to files Stickies itself creates. This is entirely optional and off by default. See the Privacy Policy for details.
